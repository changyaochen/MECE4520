Sampling variability in a regression slope

The full gas-turbine dataset is one observed collection of operating hours, not the entire population of possible operating hours. This explorer makes that idea visible by repeatedly drawing paired AT–NOx observations from the course data and fitting a new simple-regression line each time.

The draws are a visual thought experiment. They do not replace the model-based standard error developed in the slope-inference notebook; instead, they show why the fitted slope \(hat{\beta}_1\) changes from one plausible dataset to another.

TipWhat to compare

Hold the sample size fixed and increase the number of samples: the histogram becomes smoother, but its underlying spread does not shrink. Then increase the sample size: the fitted-line fan and the slope histogram should become tighter.
