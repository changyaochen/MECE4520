# MECE 4520 contributor guide

This branch is the Fall 2026 pilot redesign of the MECE 4520 course site.

- Treat `fall_2026` as the preserved snapshot of the previous course site.
- Do not restore or add content under `lectures/`; the redesigned content structure will be established deliberately.
- Keep student-facing copy clear, current, and accessible.
- Record material design decisions in `docs/FALL_2026_REDESIGN.md`.
- Avoid changing course dates, policies, staff, or published links without instructor approval.
